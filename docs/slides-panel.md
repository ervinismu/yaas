# The slides panel

Open it with the **presentation icon** in the left ribbon, or the command **Open the slides panel**. It shows the note you have open.

## At the top

- **Present** starts the slideshow.
- 🌐 opens the deck in your browser.
- ⬇ exports a PDF.

## Deck colours

One line for the whole deck:

- **Theme** sets all three colours at once. Every theme keeps its text readable.
- Three round **swatches**: background, text and highlight. Hover one to see what it colours; click it to choose a colour.
- **↺** goes back to the default colours.

A faded swatch means the colour comes from the settings, not the note.

## Each slide

Every slide has a card:

1. A top line with the slide's **number**, its **title** and a **cog**. A small dot means the slide has its own settings.
2. A **preview**. Click it to present from that slide.
3. Click the cog to open the slide's settings:
   - **Colours**: like the deck's, for this slide only
   - **Position**: middle or top
   - **Layout**: only shown when the slide has an image
   - **Backdrop**: a picture behind the slide, with **Image fit** and **Darken**
   - **Reset this slide**: removes all of its own settings

The panel writes a `%% slide … %%` comment into the slide for you and changes nothing else in the note.

The cards sit in as many columns as the panel has room for.
